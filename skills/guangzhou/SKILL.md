# Guangzhou for Westerners / 广州指南

Guangzhou (广州, Guǎngzhōu; historically "Canton") is the capital of Guangdong province and the old trading
gateway of southern China: 2,200 years as a port, home of the Canton Fair, of the wholesale markets that supply
traders from Lagos to Lima, and of the Cantonese food Westerners know as "Chinese food". It is more traditional
and more Cantonese-speaking than Shenzhen, 30 minutes away by train. This skill is a briefing for visitors,
business travellers and people relocating from Europe, North America or Australia. Figures were checked in
October 2026 and carry their date.

## Orientation
- **Scale.** 19.10 million permanent residents at end-2025, up 123,000 in the year; GDP ¥3.20 trillion in 2025
  (+4.0 %; roughly US$445bn), behind Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen and Chongqing (Guangzhou statistical bulletin,
  May 2026). Area about 7,434 km², eleven districts; with Foshan, Shenzhen and Dongguan it forms the Greater Bay
  Area 大湾区 conurbation.
- **Story.** Founded as Panyu in 214 BC; capital of the Nanyue kingdom; a Maritime Silk Road port. From
  1757 to 1842 the **Canton System** made it the only Chinese port open to Western trade; the opium trade here led to the First Opium War. Shamian Island 沙面 became the British and French
  concession; Sun Yat-sen launched the Nationalist revolution from here. The **Canton Fair** began in 1957. The 2010 Asian Games brought the Canton Tower and the new CBD.
- **Districts** (a taxi driver needs the Chinese):
  - *Tianhe* 天河: the modern centre; **Zhujiang New Town** 珠江新城 (CBD, IFC/CTF towers, Huacheng Square 花城广场,
    opera house, museum), Tianhe North 天河北 and Linhe 林和 (older offices, Guangzhou East station), Sports Centre.
  - *Yuexiu* 越秀: the historic core, city government, Beijing Road 北京路, Yuexiu Park, Guangzhou Railway Station
    广州站 and the Baima/Zhanxi wholesale clothing area; *Liwan* 荔湾: Old Xiguan 西关, Shamian Island, Shangxiajiu
    上下九, the jade and tea markets.
  - *Haizhu* 海珠: across the river; Canton Tower 广州塔 and **Pazhou** 琶洲 (Canton Fair complex, the new internet
    district with Tencent, Alibaba and Xiaomi offices); *Panyu* 番禺: Guangzhou South HSR station, Chimelong 长隆
    resort, big family estates.
  - *Baiyun* 白云: the airport and Baiyun Mountain; *Huangpu* 黄埔: Science City 科学城 (manufacturing, biotech);
    *Nansha* 南沙: port and free-trade zone.
- **Climate.** Subtropical and humid: hot May–October (32–36 °C, frequent thunderstorms), mild November–February
  (10–20 °C, with a few chilly damp spells and no central heating). Hazards: **"back-south weather"** 回南天
  (Feb–Apr: condensation on walls and floors), the **"dragon-boat rains"** 龙舟水
  (late May–June downpours and street flooding) and **typhoons** (July–September mostly). Warnings are colour-coded;
  at red, schools and offices close. Tap water is not drunk unboiled.

## Entering China and getting in
- **Visas (as of October 2026).** 30-day **unilateral visa-free entry** for ordinary passports of almost all EU
  states, Switzerland, Norway, the **UK and Canada** (since 17 February 2026), **Australia**, New Zealand, Japan,
  South Korea and others, for tourism, business, family visits and transit; the scheme currently runs to 31 December
  2026 and has been extended year by year. **US citizens are not on that list**: they need an L/M visa or use the
  **240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit** — enter at a designated port with a confirmed onward ticket to a *third*
  country or region (Hong Kong and Macau count as separate regions; a round trip back to the origin does not
  qualify). Guangzhou Baiyun airport, Pazhou ferry port (added November 2025) and Hong Kong West Kowloon station
  are designated ports; transit visitors may move within 24 provinces (65 ports nationwide). No visa on arrival.
- **Arrival card.** Since 20 November 2025 foreigners may file the arrival card online before landing (NIA site
  s.nia.gov.cn, "NIA 12367" app, WeChat/Alipay mini-program) — free; ignore paid look-alike sites. Kiosks and paper
  cards remain at the border. Visa-free travellers still file it.
- **Registration.** Hotels register you with the police; staying in a private flat, register at the local police
  station within 24 hours.
- **Baiyun International Airport (CAN)** 白云国际机场, 28 km north of the centre. **Terminal 3** opened in October
  2025; foreign airlines and most international flights moved there on 22 January 2026, and **Terminal 1 closed
  for renovation on 7 May 2026** — the airport now runs T2 (China Southern and partners) and T3. Check your
  terminal; a free 24-hour inter-terminal shuttle runs with a boarding pass.
  - **T2**: Metro **Line 3** from Airport North 机场北 to Tiyu Xilu 体育西路 (Tianhe) in about 45–50 min.
  - **T3**: the **Donghuan intercity** 广州东环城际 from Baiyun Airport East 白云机场东 (B2 of the T3 transport
    centre) to Pazhou in about 29 min (express) and on to Guangzhou South; pay with an intercity/12306 ticket or
    transit card. Or Metro Line 3/9 to Gaozeng 高增 plus a 15-minute shuttle bus.
- **From Hong Kong** (checked September 2026): **high-speed rail** from West Kowloon to Guangzhou South 广州南
  (48–85 min; ¥215 second class) or Guangzhou East 广州东 (92–108 min, fewer trains; better for Tianhe); immigration
  for both sides is done at West Kowloon; tickets go on sale about 15 days ahead. The old Hung Hom–Guangzhou East
  **through train** has not run since January 2020. Cheaper: MTR to Lo Wu, walk across, train from Shenzhen
  to Guangzhou East (2.5–3 h); coaches (3–4 h); HKIA SkyPier ferry to Pazhou for airside transfers (about 2 h).
- **The Canton Fair** 广交会 (China Import and Export Fair): twice a year at the **China Import and Export Fair
  Complex** 中国进出口商品交易会展馆 in Pazhou (Metro Line 8, Xingang Dong 新港东 and Pazhou 琶洲 stations). The
  **140th session** (autumn 2026) ran 15–19 October (electronics, machinery, vehicles, new energy, hardware),
  23–27 October (building materials, furniture, homewares, gifts) and **31 October–4 November** (textiles, apparel,
  shoes, toys, medical, food); daily hours about 09:30–18:00. The 141st (spring 2027) normally runs mid-April to early
  May; dates were not announced when checked. Pre-register as a buyer online (cantonfair.org.cn) with a passport
  scan to get the badge; hotel prices rise steeply during each phase, so book early or stay a few metro stops away.

## Money
- Currency is the renminbi (RMB, ¥, CNY); Hong Kong dollars are not accepted. No tipping.
- **Mobile payment is universal**: Alipay 支付宝 and WeChat Pay 微信支付 accept foreign Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB and
  Discover linked in the app (passport verification; set up before arrival). Typical terms (checked October 2026):
  no fee up to ¥200 per payment, 3 % above; limits after passport verification about **US$5,000 per payment and
  US$50,000 per year**; no transfers to individuals or red packets on a foreign card.
- Markets: wholesale stalls take Alipay/WeChat and cash; for large orders suppliers want a bank transfer to a company
  account (ask for a **pro-forma invoice** and a **fapiao** 发票, the official tax invoice). Foreign cards work in big
  hotels and malls; cash is legal tender; Bank of China / ICBC ATMs take foreign cards.

## Getting online and the apps that matter
- **The Great Firewall** blocks Google, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, Wikipedia, many Western news
  sites and most Western AI services. Install and test a VPN before arrival, or use **international roaming or a
  travel eSIM** (routed abroad, so not filtered) — the easiest choice for a short trip or a fair. Local SIMs (China
  Mobile, Unicom, Telecom; passport needed) are filtered.
- **Apps**: WeChat 微信 (every supplier will ask for yours), Alipay (payments, metro QR, DiDi), Amap 高德地图 or Apple
  Maps, DiDi 滴滴 (English interface), Meituan 美团 / Dianping 大众点评, Trip.com and 12306 (trains), 1688 (domestic
  wholesale prices), WeChat or Baidu camera translation for menus and price tags.

## Getting around
- **Metro** 广州地铁: 779.9 km at end-2025 (operator's annual report), with Line 13 phase 2 west and the middle of
  Line 12 due by end-2026 to pass 800 km; bilingual signs, about 06:00–23:00, fares from ¥2. Pay by Alipay/WeChat
  ride code or a Yangcheng Tong 羊城通 card. Key lines: **3**
  (airport T2–Tianhe–Panyu), **1** (Guangzhou East–Tiyu Xilu–Gongyuanqian–old city), **2** (Guangzhou Station,
  Guangzhou South), **8** (Canton Fair).
- **Taxis** are metered and cheap; drivers speak Cantonese/Mandarin only — show the Chinese address; DiDi lets you
  type in English. Motorbikes are banned in the centre; e-bike couriers are everywhere.
- **Rail**: Guangzhou South 广州南 (Panyu; HSR to Shenzhen North about 30–40 min, Hong Kong, Changsha, Wuhan,
  Beijing), Guangzhou East 广州东 (Tianhe; Shenzhen intercity, some HK trains), Guangzhou Station 广州站 (old
  centre), Guangzhou Baiyun 广州白云站 (opened December 2023). Intercity lines reach Foshan, Dongguan, Zhuhai and
  the airport. Book with your passport on 12306 or Trip.com.

## Business scene and customs
- **What Guangzhou does.** Trade (the Canton Fair, wholesale markets, Nansha and Huangpu ports), cars (GAC 广汽,
  with Honda and Toyota joint ventures; EV makers Xpeng 小鹏 and GAC Aion), retail and e-commerce (Vipshop, and
  the garment supply chain around Panyu that feeds fast-fashion platforms), games and internet (NetEase began here
  in 1997; WeChat was built at Tencent's Guangzhou R&D centre), autonomous driving (Pony.ai, WeRide robotaxis),
  China Southern Airlines.
- **Wholesale markets** (bring a translator app, small samples bag and patience; prices fall with quantity; ask
  for the MOQ 起订量 and a factory visit before a big order):
  - Clothing: **Baima** 白马服装市场 and the Zhanxi Road 站西路 malls by Guangzhou Station (mid-to-high end),
    **Shahe** 沙河 (cheap, from early morning), Thirteen Lines 十三行 (women's wear, Liwan).
  - Fabric and trims: **Zhongda** 中大布匹市场 (Haizhu), a huge textile market cluster.
  - Toys, stationery and festive goods: **Yide Road** 一德路; jade at Liwan Plaza 荔湾广场 area; tea at **Fangcun**
    芳村茶叶市场; leather bags at Shiling 狮岭 (Huadu); furniture at Lecong 乐从 (Foshan, 40 min).
- **Customs**: add WeChat first, then exchange business cards with both hands; tea and small talk before business;
  "we'll consider it" usually means no; decisions come from the boss after the meeting. Cantonese business culture is
  pragmatic, with less ceremony and lighter drinking than in the north; a seafood dinner still builds the relationship. Check a supplier's business licence 营业执照 on gsxt.gov.cn.
- **Consulates**: the US, UK, Canada, Australia, France, Germany and many others have consulates-general here, most
  in Zhujiang New Town and Tianhe; the US Consulate General handles immigrant visas for all of China.

## Living here
- **Where expats live**: **Zhujiang New Town** 珠江新城 (serviced flats, walk to the CBD, river views), **Ersha
  Island** 二沙岛 (quiet villas, the most exclusive), **Tianhe North / Linhe** 天河北 (older, convenient for Guangzhou
  East), **Panyu** 番禺 (big family estates such as Clifford Estates 祈福新邨, cheaper, near some schools), Science
  City 科学城 in Huangpu for engineers.
- **Costs** (approximate, 2026): one-bedroom in Zhujiang New Town or Tianhe ¥5,000–9,000 a month, ¥3,000–5,000 further
  out; local meals ¥20–50 and a dim-sum breakfast for two ¥80–150; Western restaurant ¥150–300. Noticeably cheaper than
  Shenzhen and far cheaper than Hong Kong. Agents (Lianjia/Beike 贝壳) charge about half a month's rent.
- **Health**: **Guangzhou United Family Hospital** 广州和睦家医院 (31 Pazhou Avenue, Haizhu; 24-hour emergency line
  020-3610 2333; English-speaking, direct billing for many international insurers); the big public teaching hospitals
  (e.g. Sun Yat-sen University First Affiliated Hospital 中山一院) have VIP clinics — call ahead for English. Bring prescriptions; dengue risk rises after summer
  rains (use repellent).
- **Schools** (foreign-passport schools; apply early, popular ones fill by spring): American International School of
  Guangzhou (AISG), Utahloy International School (full IB continuum), British School of Guangzhou (Nord Anglia),
  ISA International School of Guangzhou, BASIS International Guangzhou, Clifford School (Panyu). Tuition is roughly
  ¥150,000–330,000 a year (2026 guides; confirm with each school).
- **Daily life**: very safe; the hazards are traffic, e-bikes and weather. Cantonese helps socially but Mandarin and a
  translation app get you everywhere; English is limited outside hotels and the CBD. Its African and Middle Eastern
  trading communities (Xiaobei 小北) make it more cosmopolitan than its reputation.

## Food
Guangzhou is the capital of **Cantonese cuisine** 粤菜: fresh ingredients, light seasoning, precise heat — "the
freshest wins". Spice is rare; the opposite problem is that almost everything has pork, seafood or oyster sauce.
- **Morning tea** 早茶 / yum cha 饮茶: har gow 虾饺 (shrimp dumplings), siu mai 烧卖, rice-noodle rolls 肠粉, char siu
  bao 叉烧包, chicken feet 凤爪, egg tarts 蛋挞, with a pot of tea (tick the order sheet). Classic houses: **Panxi**
  泮溪酒家 (Liwan), **Tao Tao Ju** 陶陶居, **Guangzhou Restaurant** 广州酒家; go before 09:00 at weekends.
- **Roast meats** 烧腊: char siu 叉烧, roast goose 烧鹅, crispy pork 烧肉, white-cut chicken 白切鸡 (poached, with ginger
  and scallion). **Noodles and rice**: wonton noodles 云吞面, beef ho fun 干炒牛河, sampan congee 艇仔粥, clay-pot rice
  煲仔饭 (a winter favourite).
- **Sweets**: double-skin milk 双皮奶, ginger milk curd 姜撞奶, sweet soups 糖水; herbal tea 凉茶 is bitter. **Seafood**: pick it live from the tanks and pay by weight (confirm the price per jin 斤, 500 g,
  before it is cooked).
- Where: Shangxiajiu 上下九 and Liwan for old-school snacks, Huifu East Road 惠福东路 and Beijing Road for street food,
  Zhujiang New Town for fine dining (a Michelin Guide has covered Guangzhou since 2018). Day-trip to **Shunde** 顺德
  (Foshan), a UNESCO City of Gastronomy. Teochew and Hakka food are local too; Sichuan and Hunan restaurants supply
  heat. Say 不要猪肉 (no pork), 我对海鲜过敏 (I'm allergic to seafood), 不要味精 (no MSG); vegetarian: 素食.

## What to see (a weekend)
- **Day 1, new city**: Huacheng Square 花城广场 between the Guangdong Museum 广东省博物馆 and the Guangzhou Opera House
  (Zaha Hadid); cross to the **Canton Tower** 广州塔 (600 m; observation decks and sky-wheel); a **Pearl River night
  cruise** 珠江夜游 from Tianzi pier 天字码头 or the tower.
- **Day 2, old city**: **Chen Clan Ancestral Hall** 陈家祠 (Lingnan carving), **Shamian Island** 沙面 (colonial
  villas, banyan trees), Yongqing Fang 永庆坊 and Enning Road 恩宁路 arcades, Shangxiajiu 上下九; Yuexiu Park 越秀公园
  (Five Rams statue, Zhenhai Tower), Mausoleum of the Nanyue King 南越王博物院, Sacred Heart Cathedral 石室圣心大教堂,
  Guangxiao Temple 光孝寺, Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall 中山纪念堂.
- **More**: Baiyun Mountain 白云山 (hiking), Whampoa Military Academy site 黄埔军校旧址, Chimelong Safari Park
  长隆野生动物世界 (Panyu; children).
- **Day trips**: Foshan 佛山 (Ancestral Temple 祖庙, kung fu and lion dance heritage, Shunde food; metro-linked),
  Kaiping Diaolou 开平碉楼 (UNESCO watchtower villages, about 2 h), Shenzhen (30 min by HSR), Macau (HSR to
  Zhuhai, then walk across).
