# Chengdu for Westerners / 成都指南

Chengdu (成都, Chéngdū) is the capital of Sichuan province and the biggest city of western China: a laid-back
teahouse-and-hotpot city of 21 million that is also a serious hub for electronics, software, games and aviation.
It is the gateway to the giant pandas, the Leshan Buddha and the Tibetan plateau. English is scarcer than in
Shanghai or Shenzhen. This skill is a briefing for visitors, business travellers and people relocating from Europe,
North America or Australia. Figures were checked in October 2026 and carry their date.

## Orientation
- **Scale.** 21.54 million permanent residents at end-2025 (up 61,000); GDP ¥2.48 trillion in 2025 (+5.8 %, roughly
  US$345bn), among China's ten largest city economies (Chengdu statistical bulletin, April 2026). The municipality is
  large (about 14,300 km²) and includes farmland and mountains; the urban core is ringed by ring roads (一环 to 四环).
- **Story.** Capital of the ancient Shu 蜀 culture (the bronze masks of Sanxingdui and the gold of Jinsha, 1200–
  1000 BC); the city's name and site date from about 311 BC under the Qin, whose governor Li Bing built the
  **Dujiangyan** irrigation system (256 BC) that made the Chengdu plain "the Land of Abundance" 天府之国. Capital of
  Liu Bei's Shu Han in the Three Kingdoms; home of the poet Du Fu; the world's first paper money (jiaozi 交子, 11th
  century). A "Third Front" industrial base after 1964. The 2008 Wenchuan
  earthquake struck 80 km away.
- **Districts** (central five plus the new south):
  - *Jinjiang* 锦江: the shopping heart — Chunxi Road 春熙路, **Taikoo Li** 太古里 and Daci Temple 大慈寺, IFS (the
    climbing panda), Jiuyan Bridge 九眼桥 bars along the river.
  - *Qingyang* 青羊: Tianfu Square 天府广场, Kuanzhai Alley 宽窄巷子, People's Park 人民公园, Du Fu Thatched Cottage,
    Qingyang Palace. *Wuhou* 武侯: Wuhou Shrine and Jinli 锦里, Yulin 玉林 (cafés, bars), the Tibetan quarter by the
    shrine.
  - *Jinniu* 金牛 (north; Chengdu North station) and *Chenghua* 成华 (northeast; **panda base**, East Suburb Memory
    东郊记忆 art district, Chengdu East HSR station).
  - **High-tech Zone** 高新区 (south): Tianfu Software Park 天府软件园, Financial City 金融城, Global Center 环球中心;
    most offices and expat flats. **Tianfu New Area** 天府新区 further south (Xinglong Lake 兴隆湖, new science city).
  - Outer: Shuangliu 双流 (old airport), Jianyang 简阳 (Tianfu airport, 50 km southeast), Pidu 郫都 (universities,
    the Pixian chilli-bean paste), Dujiangyan 都江堰 (mountains, 60 km northwest).
- **Climate.** Humid and **overcast** — "Sichuan dogs bark at the sun". Summers hot and sticky (30–36 °C, heavy
  rains July–August); winters damp and chilly (3–10 °C) with **no central heating** (bring layers; flats use
  air-conditioner heat). Air quality: summer is mostly fine; **winter (November–February) smog** trapped by the
  basin often pushes the AQI above 150 — use an air purifier and an AQI app. Best months: March–May and September–November.

## Entering China and getting in from the airports
- **Visas (as of October 2026).** 30-day **unilateral visa-free entry** for ordinary passports of almost all EU
  states, Switzerland, Norway, the **UK and Canada** (since 17 February 2026), **Australia**, New Zealand, Japan,
  South Korea and others (tourism, business, family visits, transit); the scheme runs to 31 December 2026 and has
  been renewed year by year. **US citizens are not on that list**: they need an L/M visa or use the **240-hour
  (10-day) visa-free transit** — arrive at a designated port (Chengdu's airports are designated) with a confirmed
  onward ticket to a *third* country or region (Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan count as separate regions; flying
  back where you came from does not qualify); transit visitors may travel within 24 provinces. No visa on arrival.
  Tibet needs an extra Tibet Travel Permit arranged by an agency, whatever your visa.
- **Arrival card.** Since 20 November 2025 foreigners can file the arrival card online before landing (NIA site
  s.nia.gov.cn, "NIA 12367" app, WeChat/Alipay mini-program); free — avoid paid look-alike sites. Kiosks and paper
  cards remain at the airport. Visa-free travellers still file it.
- **Registration.** Hotels register you; in a private flat, register at the local police station within 24 hours.
- **Two airports — check your ticket:**
  - **Tianfu International (TFU)** 天府国际机场, opened June 2021, 50 km southeast: almost all **international**
    long-haul and most Asian flights since March 2023, plus many domestic. To town: **Metro Line 18** express from
    Tianfu Airport North 天府机场北 to Chengdu South Railway Station 火车南站 in about 37 min (fastest trains; first
    and last about 06:00–23:30), then change to Line 1; taxis 60–90 min. Metro Line 19 links the two airport areas (verify the service).
  - **Shuangliu International (CTU)** 双流国际机场, 16 km southwest: mainly domestic; since **30 July 2026** it again
    has an international hall in T1 for some **Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan** flights (Air China, Sichuan Airlines,
    Cathay, Hong Kong Airlines, Air Macau, China Airlines, EVA), which now operate from both airports. To town:
    Metro Line 10 to Taipingyuan 太平园 (change to Line 3/7), or a 30–40 min taxi.
  - Allow 2 hours between the airports; never book a connection across them without a long buffer.

## Money
- Currency is the renminbi (RMB, ¥, CNY). No tipping.
- **Mobile payment is universal**: Alipay 支付宝 and WeChat Pay 微信支付 accept foreign Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB and
  Discover linked in the app (passport verification; set up before arrival). Typical terms (checked October 2026):
  no fee up to ¥200 per payment, 3 % above; limits about **US$5,000 per payment and US$50,000 per year**; no
  transfers to individuals or red packets on a foreign card.
- Small restaurants, teahouses and street stalls are QR-only in practice; foreign cards work in international hotels
  and big malls (IFS, Taikoo Li). Cash is legal tender — carry some small notes for temples and markets; Bank of China
  / ICBC ATMs take foreign cards. Ask for a **fapiao** 发票 (tax invoice) for expenses.

## Getting online and the apps that matter
- **The Great Firewall** blocks Google, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, Wikipedia, many Western news sites
  and most Western AI services. Install and test a VPN before arrival, or use **international roaming or a travel
  eSIM** (routed abroad, not filtered). Local SIMs (China Mobile, Unicom, Telecom; passport needed) are filtered.
- **Apps**: WeChat 微信 (messaging, payments, mini-programs, panda-base tickets), Alipay (payments, metro QR, DiDi),
  Amap 高德地图 or Apple Maps, DiDi 滴滴 (English interface), Meituan 美团 / Dianping 大众点评 (food, reviews, hotpot
  queues), Trip.com and 12306 (trains), "Tianfu Tong" 天府通 (metro and bus card/QR), WeChat or Baidu camera
  translation for menus.

## Getting around
- **Metro** 成都地铁: Chengdu is China's fourth-largest urban-rail network — over 750 km after Lines 13 and 30 opened on
  16 December 2025 (operator figure; the national ministry's count is about 720 km); bilingual signs; fares from ¥2,
  distance-based; about 06:00–23:00 (check last trains). Useful lines: **1** (north–south: North station, Tianfu
  Square, South station, Financial City/Software Park), **2** (east–west: Chengdu East HSR, Chunxi Road, Tianfu
  Square), **3** (panda base side, Chunxi Road, Taipingyuan), **18** (Tianfu airport express), **10** (Shuangliu).
- **Taxis and DiDi** are cheap; drivers speak Sichuanese/Mandarin — show the Chinese address. Shared bikes everywhere;
  e-scooters ride on pavements.
- **Rail**: **Chengdu East** 成都东站 (HSR to Chongqing about 1–1.5 h, Xi'an about 3–4 h, Leshan/Emei, Guiyang),
  **Chengdu South** 成都南站 (Leshan, Emei), Chengdu Station 成都站 (north, older lines), Chengdu West 成都西站
  (Ya'an). Book with passport on 12306 or Trip.com. The **China–Europe Railway Express**
  freight trains leave from the Qingbaijiang 青白江 international railway port.

## Business scene and customs
- **Why it matters.** The economic capital of western China and twin pole (with Chongqing) of the Chengdu–Chongqing
  economic circle. Strengths:
  - *Electronics*: Intel's assembly and test plant, Foxconn (iPads), Texas Instruments, BOE displays.
  - *Software, games and animation*: Tianfu Software Park hosts studios and R&D teams of Tencent, Alibaba, ByteDance,
    NetEase and many indie game firms; Chengdu's **Coco Cartoon** 可可豆动画 and director Jiaozi made *Ne Zha 2*
    (2025), China's highest-grossing film. A big pool of engineering graduates (UESTC 电子科技大学, Sichuan
    University) and salaries well below the coast.
  - *Aviation and defence*: Chengdu Aircraft (J-20), Air China and Sichuan Airlines hubs.
  - *Finance and services*: regional HQs in Financial City; *biotech* in Tianfu International Bio-Town; consumer
    brands (the hotpot chain Haidilao was founded in Sichuan's Jianyang in 1994).
- **Fairs**: China (Western) International Fair for Investment and Trade (yearly; dates vary — check), the China Food
  and Drinks Fair 糖酒会 (its spring session is usually in Chengdu).
- **Customs**: add WeChat first; business cards with both hands; tea and small talk before business; indirect "no"s;
  decisions from the top. Chengdu is relaxed — long lunches, mahjong 麻将 and teahouse meetings are part of business,
  and dinner is usually **hotpot** (tell the host your spice limit early; it is not rude). Baijiu 白酒 toasts happen
  but are lighter than in the north. Punctuality is expected of foreigners.
- **Consulates**: the UK, Germany, France and about twenty other countries have consulates-general here. The **US
  consulate closed in July 2020**; US citizens in the region use the Embassy in Beijing.

## Living here
- **Where expats live**: the **High-tech Zone** 高新区 around Financial City 金融城 and Tianfu Software Park (modern
  towers, near offices and schools), **Wuhou / Yulin** 玉林 and the south 2nd–3rd ring (older, leafier, cafés and bars),
  **Jinjiang** near Taikoo Li (central, lively), and villa compounds in Tianfu New Area for families.
- **Costs** (approximate, 2026): one-bedroom in the High-tech Zone or centre ¥3,000–5,500 a month; local meals
  ¥15–40; hotpot ¥80–150 a head; Western dinner ¥120–250. Among the cheapest of China's big cities — rents roughly half
  of Shanghai's. Agents (Lianjia/Beike 贝壳) charge about half a month's rent.
- **Health**: **West China Hospital** 华西医院 (Sichuan University) is one of China's top hospitals, with a VIP /
  international clinic; Chengdu First People's Hospital 成都市第一人民医院 runs a medical centre for foreigners;
  private clinics with English-speaking doctors operate in the High-tech Zone (call ahead; many expats carry
  international insurance). Bring prescriptions; the "Health Chengdu" app books public hospitals in Chinese.
- **Schools** (foreign-passport schools; apply early): Chengdu International School (American curriculum, K–12), QSI
  International School of Chengdu, Léman International School, BASIS International Chengdu (bilingual); Malvern
  College Chengdu (British boarding, Xinjin). Tuition roughly ¥150,000–300,000 a year (2026 guides; confirm).
- **Daily life**: very safe and relaxed — the city prides itself on "slow living" 慢生活: tea in the park, mahjong,
  a creative scene, and great hiking two hours away. Hazards: traffic, e-scooters, winter smog. English is limited outside hotels, the
  High-tech Zone and Taikoo Li; a translation app is essential.

## Food
Chengdu was named a **UNESCO City of Gastronomy** in 2010, the first in Asia; a Michelin Guide has covered it since
2022. Sichuan food is not just hot: the signature is **málà** 麻辣, numbing Sichuan pepper 花椒 plus chilli, and the
cuisine claims "a hundred dishes, a hundred flavours" (fish-fragrant, strange-flavour, sweet-and-sour, tea-smoked).
- **Spice levels** — say them clearly when ordering: **不辣** bú là (no chilli), **微辣** wēi là (mild), **中辣** zhōng là
  (medium), **特辣 / 重辣** tè là (very hot); **少麻** shǎo má (less numbing). Even 微辣 is hot by Western standards.
  "No spice at all" may need 一点都不要辣 and a non-Sichuan dish.
- **Classics**: mapo tofu 麻婆豆腐 (Chen Mapo 陈麻婆豆腐, since 1862), kung pao chicken 宫保鸡丁, twice-cooked pork
  回锅肉, fish-fragrant shredded pork 鱼香肉丝, water-boiled fish 水煮鱼, cold "mouth-watering chicken" 口水鸡,
  husband-and-wife beef slices 夫妻肺片 (beef and offal), tea-smoked duck 樟茶鸭. Mild options: Zhong dumplings 钟水饺
  (sweet soy), Long wontons 龙抄手 in clear broth, sweet-water noodles 甜水面, dan dan noodles 担担面 (can be ordered
  mild), leaf-wrapped glutinous rice 叶儿粑.
- **Hotpot** 火锅: order a **split pot** 鸳鸯锅 (yuānyang: spicy and clear broth); dip in sesame oil with garlic;
  typical items beef tripe 毛肚, duck intestine 鸭肠, potato slices, lotus root. Cousins: chuanchuan 串串香 (skewers,
  pay per stick), mao cai 冒菜 (hotpot in a bowl), bobo chicken 钵钵鸡 (cold skewers in chilli oil), and the local
  snack of spicy **rabbit head** 兔头.
- **Where**: Yulin 玉林 and Jianshe Road 建设路 for street food; Kuanzhai Alley and Jinli are touristy but easy;
  Taikoo Li for modern Sichuan and Western food. Teahouses: **Heming** 鹤鸣茶社 in People's Park (cheap gaiwan tea,
  ear-cleaners 采耳 at work). Say 我对花生过敏 (allergic to peanuts — common in kung pao and street sauces), 我吃素
  (vegetarian).

## What to see (a weekend)
- **Pandas**: the **Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding** 成都大熊猫繁育研究基地 (northeast, Chenghua):
  ¥55 adults; March–October entry 07:30–17:00, November–February 08:00–16:30 (official site, checked October 2026);
  real-name booking online up to 14 days ahead with your passport (the WeChat system can reject foreign numbers —
  Trip.com works); arrive at opening, pandas sleep after mid-morning; internal shuttle bus ¥30.
- **Day 1, old Chengdu**: People's Park 人民公园 teahouse and the "marriage market", Kuanzhai Alley 宽窄巷子, Wenshu
  Monastery 文殊院 (vegetarian lunch), Wuhou Shrine 武侯祠 and the Jinli 锦里 lane, a Sichuan opera **face-changing**
  变脸 show in the evening (Shufeng Yayun 蜀风雅韵 in the Culture Park).
- **Day 2, culture and modern**: Du Fu Thatched Cottage 杜甫草堂, **Jinsha Site Museum** 金沙遗址博物馆 (Golden Sun
  Bird), Sichuan Museum, Taikoo Li 太古里 and Daci Temple at night, Jiuyan Bridge 九眼桥 bars by the river.
- **Day trips** (verify current tickets):
  - **Leshan Giant Buddha** 乐山大佛: the 71-m Tang-dynasty Buddha carved into a cliff; HSR from Chengdu East/South
    to Leshan about 1 h, then bus or taxi; a river boat shows the whole figure. Combine with Mount **Emei** 峨眉山
    (3,099 m, Buddhist temples, monkeys; UNESCO with Leshan since 1996) as an overnight.
  - **Mount Qingcheng** 青城山 (Taoist birthplace, forest trails) and **Dujiangyan** 都江堰 (the working 256 BC
    irrigation system): UNESCO since 2000; intercity train from Chengdu Station 成都站 or Xipu 犀浦 to Qingchengshan/Dujiangyan in
    about 1 h; one long day for both. Dujiangyan's Panda Valley 熊猫谷 and the Dujiangyan panda base offer quieter panda
    visits (the "volunteer" programmes are fee-based; verify).
  - **Sanxingdui Museum** 三星堆博物馆 (Guanghan, about 1 h north; bronze masks, new museum building 2023).
  - Further: Jiuzhaigou 九寨沟 (flight or HSR; 2–3 days), the Tibetan plateau (altitude), Chongqing (HSR).
