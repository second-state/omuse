SINGAPORE AIRLINES   Baggage services

Delayed, damaged or lost baggage

Last updated 1 October 2026

1. Report it before you leave the airport

Our baggage services counter files a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) and gives you a file reference (for example NRTSQ12345).

2. Track your bag

Track your delayed bag online with your file reference and family name on WorldTracer baggage tracing.

3. Interim expenses

We reimburse reasonable essential purchases (toiletries, a change of clothes) while your bag is delayed, against original receipts. Email your claim with the file reference, your itemised receipts and boarding pass to baggage.claims@singaporeair.com.sg. Do not send bank details by email; we contact you for them.

4. Lost baggage

If your bag has not been found within 21 days, it is considered lost and you can claim for its contents. For international journeys our liability is limited under the Montreal Convention.
