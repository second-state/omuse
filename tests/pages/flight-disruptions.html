Delays and cancellations

Last updated 1 October 2026

If your flight is delayed

  • Delays of more than 3 hours: meal vouchers at the airport.
  • Delays of more than 5 hours, or a cancellation: you may rebook free of charge on the next available Singapore Airlines or Scoot flight, or request a full refund of the unused ticket to your original form of payment. A travel voucher is offered as an alternative, not instead of a refund.
  • Overnight delays: hotel accommodation and transfers are arranged by our airport staff.

Flight delay certificate

For your travel insurer: ask our airport staff, or email delay.certificate@singaporeair.com.sg with your booking reference, name and flight number. We send it within 3 working days.

Your rights

For flights departing the European Union or the United Kingdom, EU Regulation 261/2004 (or UK261) also applies. Keep every receipt for meals and transport while you wait.
