Lost or stolen passports

Page last updated: 3 September 2026

In an emergency outside office hours, call the 24-hour Consular Emergency Centre in Canberra: +61 2 6261 3305.

What to do

  1. Report the loss or theft to the nearest police box (kōban) or police station and ask for the report receipt number (遺失届受理番号).
  2. Report the passport lost or stolen online or with us — once reported, it is cancelled and cannot be used again.
  3. Make an appointment to apply for a replacement passport or, if you need to travel urgently, an emergency passport.

What to bring to your appointment

  • the police report receipt number
  • two identical passport photos (45 mm × 35 mm, taken in the last 6 months)
  • a completed application form and the lost/stolen passport statement
  • a copy of your lost passport, if you have one, and proof of your onward travel (flight booking)

Emergency passports are usually issued within 2 working days.

Consular section — opening hours

Monday to Friday, 09:00–12:30 and 13:30–16:00, by appointment (closed on Japanese and Australian public holidays).
