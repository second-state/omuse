Visa Exemption Scheme for tourism and short business visits

Updated 1 October 2026

From 15 September 2026, nationals of the countries and territories listed below may enter Thailand without a visa for tourism, short business meetings or visiting family, and stay for up to 30 days per entry (previously 60 days). Travellers who entered on or before 14 September 2026 keep the period stamped on entry. The stay may be extended once by up to 30 days at a local immigration office (fee THB 1,900).

Conditions at the border

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.
  • A confirmed onward or return ticket leaving Thailand within the permitted stay.
  • Funds of at least THB 20,000 per person (THB 40,000 per family) may be requested.
  • All foreign nationals must submit the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) at tdac.immigration.go.th within 3 days before arrival. The TDAC is free.

Countries and territories (selection)

Andorra, Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Brazil, Brunei, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong SAR, Iceland, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Republic of Korea, Kuwait, Laos, Luxembourg, Macao SAR, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan, Türkiye, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States of America, Viet Nam.

Holders of other passports apply for a visa online at thaievisa.go.th before travelling.
