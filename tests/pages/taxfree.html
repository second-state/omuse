Consumption tax refund for visitors — the new procedure

Updated 15 October 2026

From 1 November 2026 Japan's tax-free system changes to a refund system: you pay the tax-inclusive price in the shop and receive the consumption tax back when you leave Japan.

In the shop

  • Show your passport (the original, not a copy). The shop scans it and records the purchase.
  • You pay the full price including the 10% consumption tax. The upper limit for consumables and the sealed-bag rule no longer apply.

At departure

  • Scan your passport at a tax-free terminal at the airport before checking in your luggage, with the goods and receipts in hand.
  • Green: confirmed. Red: show the goods at the customs counter.
  • Only goods purchased within 90 days before departure qualify.
  • All items on one receipt must be presented — if any item is missing, the refund for that receipt is not paid.

The refund

The tax is refunded after departure to the card or account you chose in the shop's system (the shop or its refund agent may charge a fee).

Purchases up to 31 October 2026 follow the previous tax-exemption procedure at the shop.
