Narita Express (N'EX)

Timetable valid from the 14 March 2026 timetable revision. Updated 1 October 2026.

All seats on the Narita Express are reserved. Narita Airport Terminal 1 to Tokyo in about 53 minutes, no change of trains. Each car has a luggage area with combination locks for large suitcases.

Tickets: JR East ticket machines and JR EAST Travel Service Centers at both airport stations, or online at the JR-EAST Train Reservation site (from one month before travel). Suica can be used with a seat reservation bought separately.

Luggage

  • Large suitcases go in the luggage area at the end of each car (lockable with a combination of your choice).
  • Elevators at Narita Airport and Tokyo stations; at Tokyo the N'EX arrives underground at platforms 1–4 (Sobu Line, B5F).
