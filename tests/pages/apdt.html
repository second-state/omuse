Notes for Application

Under section 12 of the Air Passenger Departure Tax Ordinance (Cap. 140) (“the Ordinance”), a passenger of any description or class mentioned in the Second Schedule to the Ordinance shall be exempted from the Air Passenger Departure Tax (“APDT”). An eligible passenger who has paid the APDT when purchasing the air ticket may apply for the refund of APDT. The following passengers exempted from the APDT may apply for refund via this Refund Application Platform –

(i) Passengers who arrive at and depart from Hong Kong by air on the same day or the following calendar day; and

(ii) Passengers arriving by land or sea and departing by aircraft on the same day or on the following day, except those who have left Hong Kong by land or sea on the day or the preceding day of their departure flights.

Anytime, Anywhere

Eligible passengers should submit a tax refund application via this e-platform within 28 calendar days from the date of their departure flight from Hong Kong.

Passengers travelling via the SkyPier Terminal by ferry or coach should claim their refund at the “Departure Tax Refund Desk” at the SkyPier Terminal.

From 03 November 2026, passengers arriving at Hong Kong International Airport by SkyPier Terminal cross-boundary ferry or coach or using Park & Fly service should submit their refund applications via this Refund Application Platform.

Passengers who have left Hong Kong through immigration controls other than by aircraft on the day of their departure flight or the preceding day are not eligible for the APDT exemption.

Personal Information Collection Statement (“PICS”)

The personal information provided by you as the applicant or on behalf of the applicant (as his/her Relevant Person[1] with parental responsibility), as applicable, will be used for the purpose of processing your or the applicant’s application for refund of Air Passenger Departure Tax. It may be disclosed to the relevant departments of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (“HKSARG”), the Airport Authority and its authorized agents and/or contractors who are responsible for handling the refund. The information provided will be destroyed when it is no longer required.

Your or the applicant’s application will not be considered if you fail to provide all the requested information or document.

You have the right to request access to and correction of personal data provided in accordance with the provisions of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486). Such request should be made in writing to the Airport Authority.

[1] According to section 2 of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), the term “Relevant Person” means a person who has parental responsibility for a minor (i.e. a person who has not attained the age of 18 years).

Boarding passes

Boarding pass of the departure flight from Hong Kong, and the boarding pass of the relevant arrival flight in Hong Kong (passengers arriving by air), physical or electronic.

Proof of tax payment

Receipt, itinerary or air ticket issued by an airline or travel agent showing the amount of APDT paid. If you do not have such proof, please contact your airline or travel agent for assistance.
