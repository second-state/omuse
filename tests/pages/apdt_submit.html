Proceed with Facial Recognition

Completion of departure procedures is required to confirm your eligibility for a tax refund.

Please complete all departure procedures at Hong Kong International Airport before proceeding with facial recognition; otherwise, your application may fail.

Do not proceed with facial recognition if departure procedures are incomplete.

If departure procedures are incomplete, please click Exit and resume your application after completing departure procedures.

Facial recognition compares your face with the photo on the travel document you uploaded. It uses the front camera of your mobile phone: please keep your face centered in the screen and complete the actions shown (blink, open your mouth, turn your head).
