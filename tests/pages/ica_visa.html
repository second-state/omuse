Visa Requirements

Last updated 20 September 2026

Citizens of Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States, the member states of the European Union and many other countries do not need a visa to visit Singapore for social visits or short business meetings. Holders of passports of the countries in Assessment Level I and II must apply for a visa before travelling.

Before you travel

  • Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.
  • All travellers, including Singapore citizens, submit the SG Arrival Card with the electronic health declaration at eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard within 3 days before arrival. It is free of charge — beware of websites that charge a fee.
  • Have a confirmed onward or return ticket, and enough funds for your stay.

On arrival

The duration of stay is decided at the checkpoint and shown in your e-Pass notification sent to the email address in your SG Arrival Card. Eligible foreign visitors may use the automated lanes at Changi Airport.
